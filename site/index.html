Installation

Requires macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later. Prebuilt releases target Apple Silicon; Intel Macs can build from source.

Download Prebuilt App

Grab the latest .dmg or .zip bundle directly from GitHub Releases and drag GTV Remote.app into /Applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GTV Remote signed and notarized by Apple?

Yes. Official releases from v1.0.5 onward are signed with an Apple Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple, so they open without Gatekeeper warnings.

Why isn't my Google TV / Android TV showing up in the device list?

Ensure your Mac and TV are on the same Wi-Fi subnet, grant Local Network permission under System Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network, and verify Android TV Remote Service is enabled on the TV.

How do Quick Apps and custom deep links work?

Quick Apps send deep link URIs (https:// or app://) directly over the Android TV Remote v2 protocol. You can reorder presets, toggle visibility, or add custom apps in Quick Apps Settings, and launch positions 1–9 via number keys.

Does GTV Remote collect any data or connect to external servers?

No. GTV Remote communicates exclusively over your local network with your TV using mutual TLS and a locally generated RSA-2048 certificate. There are zero analytics, telemetry, or external server calls.

Which TVs and macOS versions are supported?

Any TV or streaming box supporting Google TV / Android TV Remote v2 (Chromecast with Google TV, Sony Bravia, TCL, Hisense, Nvidia Shield, etc.) and Macs running macOS 14.0 Sonoma or newer (Apple Silicon for prebuilt releases; Intel Macs via building from source).
